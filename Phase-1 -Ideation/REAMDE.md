# Phase 1 – Ideation

## Problem Statement
Coaches and team managers face difficulty in tracking cricket player performance accurately because player data is stored in different files and formats.

## Empathy Map
### Users
- Team Managers
- Coaches
- Analysts
- Salesforce Admins

### Pain Points
- Data errors
- Delays in collecting statistics
- Difficulty in analyzing player performance

### Needs
- Accurate player data
- Better performance insights
- Faster decision making
- Automated data management

## Brainstorming
The proposed ideas included:
- Web-based player management system
- Mobile statistics tracker
- Salesforce-integrated CRM
- AI-based performance prediction
- Dashboard-based analytics

## Selected Idea
A Salesforce-based Cricket Player Performance Tracking Platform named **CRICKSTATS**.
